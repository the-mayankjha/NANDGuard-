# ⚙️ 2️⃣ Why Companies Don’t Instantly Implement ML in Firmware

## 🔹 A) Firmware Has Resource Constraints

SSD controllers:

- Limited RAM
- Limited CPU
- Strict real-time constraints
- Must be ultra-reliable

Running heavy ML models inside firmware:

- Risky
- Expensive
- Hard to validate

Firmware prioritizes:

> Stability over experimentation.

---

## 🔹 B) Safety-Critical Systems Avoid Rapid Changes

Storage devices:

- Cannot afford unpredictable behavior
- Must pass strict validation
- Must survive millions of devices in production

Adding ML:

- Requires huge validation cycles
- Requires regulatory & reliability certification

So companies move slowly.

---

## 🔹 C) Most Systems Are Still Threshold-Based

Even in 2025:

Many industrial systems still rely on:

- Statistical heuristics
- Rule engines
- Manual tuning

Because:

- They are predictable
- Easy to debug
- Deterministic

AI models:

- Are probabilistic
- Harder to interpret
- Require dataset maintenance

---

## 🔹 D) Enterprise AI Layer vs Firmware AI

Companies often prefer:

Firmware → stable, rule-based
AI layer → external monitoring system

Because:

- Easier to update
- Doesn’t risk hardware stability
- Scalable via cloud

That’s where our solution fits.
